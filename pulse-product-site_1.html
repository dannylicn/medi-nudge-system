The real problem

It's not forgetting. It's "playing doctor."

We started thinking patients forget. We were wrong.

Interviews revealed the real behaviour: patients underestimate the risk of a missed dose. They self-diagnose — "skipping once won't matter" — and silently break the treatment plan. Caregivers don't know. Doctors don't know. Until something goes wrong.

How it works

From a missed pill to a safer patient — together.

Pulse closes the loop between patient, caregiver, and the doctor. Every dose becomes a data point. Every miss becomes a chance to intervene — and to learn.

How we measure success

Three numbers we live by.

If Pulse is working, these are the metrics that prove it — across health outcomes, behaviour change, and daily habit.

Outcome
≥80%
Patients reaching the clinical adherence threshold

The clinical standard for "adherent." We measure the share of enrolled chronic patients sustaining ≥80% medication adherence rate (MAR) over a 30-day window.

Why it matters → Below this line, treatment efficacy drops sharply and hospitalisation risk climbs.
Behavioural
Δ+
Adherence lift after caregiver or AI intervention

% of patients whose adherence improves in the 7 days following a caregiver nudge or an AI-generated alert. Tests whether the escalation loop actually changes behaviour.

Why it matters → Validates the core thesis: that timely intervention moves the needle.
Engagement
DAU/MAU
Stickiness across patient and caregiver apps

Daily over monthly active users — tracked separately for the patient and caregiver experiences. A chronic condition is daily, so the app must be too.

Why it matters → If engagement fades, every downstream metric will follow.
